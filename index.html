Ride Notes
Before You Roll Out
🕐

Best Time

Weekday afternoons. Less traffic on Ocean Ave, emptier bars, and the Henry Hudson Trail is calm. Avoid Route 35 between Asbury and Bradley Beach on summer weekends.

🚲

Trail Info

Every leg has a bike-safe route — Ocean Ave has protected lanes in Long Branch, Route 71 has wide shoulders through Deal, and the Henry Hudson Trail is a paved car-free path for the Sea Bright to Red Bank stretch. Click any route link for turn-by-turn directions.

🔒

Bike Parking

Asbury Park has bike corrals on Bond St and bike share stations downtown. Red Bank has a bike repair station at Borough Hall (90 Monmouth St). Carry a U-lock.

🍺

Pace Yourself

41 miles is a real ride. Alternate water with beer. Eat at every stop — the food at these places is part of the experience. Nip N Tuck's grilled cheeses and The Ark's steamers are non-negotiable.

🗺️

Alternate Return

Short on time? Skip Donovan's Reef and ride straight from The Ark back to Oceanport via Route 88 and local streets. Cuts ~8 miles off the total.

🌧️

Rain Check

Every stop on this route is worthy on its own. If weather turns, pick 2-3 and Uber between them. The Ark in Point Pleasant and Nip N Tuck in Long Branch are both solid rainy day picks.
