Ride Notes
Before You Roll Out
🕐

Best Time

Weekday afternoons. Less traffic on Ocean Ave, emptier bars, and the Henry Hudson Trail is calm. Avoid Route 35 between Asbury and Bradley Beach on summer weekends.

🚲

Trail Info

The Henry Hudson Trail is 24 miles, paved, flat, and part of the National Rails-to-Trails Network. Use it for the return leg from Sea Bright through Atlantic Highlands back to Oceanport.

🔒

Bike Parking

Asbury Park has bike corrals on Bond St and bike share stations downtown. Red Bank has a bike repair station at Borough Hall (90 Monmouth St). Carry a U-lock.

🍺

Pace Yourself

33 miles is a real ride. Alternate water with beer. Eat at every stop — the food at these places is part of the experience. Nip N Tuck's grilled cheeses and The Ark's steamers are non-negotiable.

🗺️

Alternate Return

Short on time? Skip Donovan's Reef and ride straight from The Ark back to Oceanport via Route 88 and local streets. Cuts ~8 miles off the total.

🌧️

Rain Check

Every stop on this route is a-worthy on its own. If weather turns, pick 2-3 and Uber between them. The Globe in Red Bank (20 E Front St) is a solid substitute for Donovan's if you're staying inland.
