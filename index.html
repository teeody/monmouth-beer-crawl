Every Pour on the Route
Dive bars, craft breweries, and a 184-year-old sports bar — all connected by the best bike-friendly roads in Monmouth County. A 28-mile loop from Oceanport down the coast to Bradley Beach, then inland to Red Bank and back. No spandex required.

Ride Notes
Before You Roll Out
🕐

Best Time

Start at Birdsmouth when they open at noon. You've got 28 miles ahead — hitting the first few bars early means less crowds and more barstools. The Globe in Red Bank is open until 2am, so you've got all afternoon and evening to pace yourself.

🚲

Trail Info

Every leg has a bike-safe route — Ocean Ave has protected lanes in Long Branch, Route 71 has wide shoulders through Deal, Branch Ave has a dedicated bike lane, and Navesink River Rd is scenic and low-traffic for the final push to Red Bank. Click any route link for turn-by-turn directions.

🔒

Bike Parking

Asbury Park has bike corrals on Bond St and bike share stations downtown. Red Bank has a bike repair station at Borough Hall (90 Monmouth St). Carry a U-lock.

🍺

Pace Yourself

28 miles is a real ride. Alternate water with beer. Eat at every stop — the food at these places is part of the experience. Nip N Tuck's grilled cheeses and The Globe's burgers are non-negotiable.

🗺️

Alternate Return

Short on time? Skip The Globe and ride straight from Erratic Fermentations back to Oceanport via Route 35. Cuts ~7 miles off the total.

🌧️

Rain Check

Every stop on this route is worthy on its own. If weather turns, pick 2-3 and Uber between them. The Globe in Red Bank and Nip N Tuck in Long Branch are both solid rainy day picks.
